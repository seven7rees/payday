# PAYDAY: Workshop listing (final wording, not yet published)

## In the builder's words

Launch your coin. Keep half. Pay your holders every week. Your cut as launcher: 50% of every creator fee, paid straight from the split. No code, no server, nothing to run. Buys have no extra hook fee. A sell within 10 minutes on the wallet's holding clock sends 0.8% of its SOL into the pot, with no extra early-sell fee after that. Normal trading fees still apply. The other half of the fee fills the pot, and 75% of the pot pays holders in SOL every week. Hold 1,000+ coins through the full week (weeks start Thursday, 00:00 UTC) and get paid the next week, with no claiming. Hold longer, earn more: 1x from 6 hours, 1.5x from 7 days, 2x from 30 days, +10% if the holding never sold. The full split, no fine print: 50% of the fee to you, 50% to the pot. Of the pot, 75% goes to holders and 25% to the hook builder.

## Goes to

Holders 75% / Hook builder 25% of the pot.

Separately, every creator fee is split 50% to the coin launcher and 50% into the pot. The hook builder's cut comes out of the pot, not the launcher's half.

## Rules

Buys have no extra hook fee. A sell within 10 minutes on the wallet's holding clock sends 0.8% of its SOL proceeds into the pot. The early-sell window is based on the wallet's holding clock, not time since launch. There is no extra early-sell fee after that. Normal trading and platform fees still apply.

## Details

Weeks start Thursday at 00:00 UTC. Eligible wallets hold at least 1,000 coins through the full week. Only the balance held through that week counts; mid-week buys and top-ups don't increase that week's payout weight.

Hold-time weights: 1x from 6 hours, 1.5x from 7 days, 2x from 30 days, plus 10% if the holding never sold. Payouts go directly to wallets during the following week, with no claiming. Payouts depend on trading.

## Status

These are the current repo's settings. Live deployment and the platform's own checks have not been verified. The code sets the hook's early-sell fee at 0.8% and describes a platform add-on (about 1% total for the seller); confirm that add-on before publishing the total.
