Creator fee: half to the launcher, half to the pot. Builder: a quarter of new pot money; the rest is weekly SOL for holders. A share is a wallet's lowest week balance (1,000+ coins) by hold time at the week's start: 1x at 6h, 1.5x at 7d, 2x at 30d, +10% if unsold when counted. Paid the next week; going under 1,000 coins first forfeits it. Shares under 0.0001 SOL or a wallet can't take stay in the pot. The launcher isn't paid as a holder and can change payout limits. Senders get a pot tip. Hook: 1% of a sell in its first 10 min of averaged hold; buys: no hook fee, launch fees apply.
